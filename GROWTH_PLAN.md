# Portfolio Growth Plan

## Purpose

A concrete, shippable plan to get organic traffic to `appcatalyst.org`.
Built around what Chase actually solves, not what the clients happen to be.

---

## Step 0 — Define the real niche (before anything else)

The apps Chase has built so far are in insurance, medical sales, card
collecting, and other verticals — but those are **accidents of who
walked through the door**, not the niche. The real niche is the kind
of problem Chase solves, the kind of person he's best for, and the
edge he has that other developers don't.

Filling this in correctly is the foundation for:

- Home page hero copy
- SEO keyword strategy (what we want to rank for)
- Which industry / audience landing pages to build
- How to write every blog post going forward
- What to say on LinkedIn / X when sharing posts

If this part is wrong, every downstream decision drifts.

### Questions for Chase to answer

Answer in your own words — short bullets are fine. Don't try to sound
marketing-y; say what's actually true.

1. **Who is the person I most want more of as a client?**
   Describe the role, the stage of their business, what they do every day,
   what they already spent money on before finding me.

2. **What is the SAME underlying problem I end up solving, across
   every app I've shipped?**
   Not the surface ("they needed an insurance app"). The deeper pattern
   ("they had tribal knowledge that wasn't scaling," "they were losing
   reps because onboarding was chaos," etc.).

3. **What do clients thank me for that a random developer wouldn't
   have done?**
   The moments where someone said "I didn't expect that," or "no one else
   did that for me." Those are your edge.

4. **What kind of projects do I turn down, or wish I had?**
   A niche is defined as much by what you say no to as what you say yes to.

5. **If a happy client recommended me to a friend over dinner, how
   would they describe what I do in one sentence?**
   Not what you wrote on LinkedIn. What they'd actually say.

6. **What's unique about HOW I work that solves their problem?**
   Speed? Price? Full ownership of their code? Shipping before the
   contract is signed? Being reachable on iMessage? Something else?

## Step 1 — Game plan (A through J)

_Will be generated once Step 0 is filled in. Locking the plan to the
real niche first so we don't build the wrong landing pages, capture
the wrong emails, or chase the wrong keywords._

### Scope preview (final wording depends on Step 0)

- **A.** Email capture at end of every blog post + home hero + footer
- **B.** Share buttons on every blog post (X, LinkedIn, copy-link)
- **C.** Related posts at end of each blog post
- **D.** Sitemap.xml + robots.txt audit + JSON-LD Article schema
- **E.** Lead magnet PDF gated behind email
- **F.** Auto-generated OG image per blog post
- **G.** Rewrite home hero + SEO meta for niche positioning
- **H.** Rework `/projects` page to group by the niche's verticals
- **I.** Niche-specific landing pages for SEO long-tail capture
- **J.** Case study pages for 2-3 flagship apps

### First-week recommendation

Once Step 0 is locked, ship **G + A + D** first. Highest leverage per
hour, unlocks every downstream piece.

---

## Rules of the road

- No generic "I build apps" positioning — that's a red ocean.
- Every page change must serve either **traffic** or **conversion**,
  not vanity.
- Every blog post going forward gets CTA + share + related.
- SEO plumbing before SEO content — no point writing more posts if
  Google can't parse them.
